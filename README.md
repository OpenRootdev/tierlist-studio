## What it does

Drop your pictures onto the board and drag them into tiers. When it looks right, save it as a PNG or copy it straight into a post.

- Items snap neatly into place, or you can turn snapping off and put them anywhere.
- Write a short note under any picture, rename tiers and pick their colours.
- Change the whole look: fonts, colours, sizes, borders and rounded corners, with four ready-made styles.

## Get started

1. Open <a href="https://openrootdev.github.io/tierlist-studio/">openrootdev.github.io/tierlist-studio</a>. There is nothing to install.
2. Or download <a href="https://github.com/OpenRootdev/tierlist-studio/releases/latest/download/tierlist-studio.zip">tierlist-studio.zip</a>, unzip it and open <code>index.html</code>.
3. Drop your pictures in, drag them into tiers, then click <b>Export PNG</b>.

```bash
git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/OpenRootdev/tierlist-studio
```

## What you need

- A recent browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
- An internet connection when you open it, for the fonts and the picture export.
- Your own pictures. They stay on your computer and are never uploaded.
